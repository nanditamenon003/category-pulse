Category Pulse is a free prototype built by one person as a personal project. By using it, you agree to these terms.

### It's a prototype

- It has been tested on simulated stores and made-up reports, not on a real store's data.
- It's provided as it is, with no promise that it will be accurate, always available or kept running. It may change or stop at any time, so keep your own copies of your files.
- On free hosting, the app can go to sleep and may take a little time to wake.

### Check before you act

Causes, forecasts, plans and stock quantities are estimates worked out from the data you give it. AI chat answers can also be wrong. Check them before making decisions about stock, staff or money. Those decisions remain yours, and the person running the project isn't responsible for losses that follow from them.

### Your data, your responsibility

- Only upload data you have the right to use. If it belongs to your employer or contains anyone's personal details, get permission first.
- Your data stays yours. See the Privacy page for how it's handled.
- Don't use the AI chat on confidential figures. It sends what it looks up to an outside company, after asking you.

### Fair use

Please don't try to get around the sign-in or the chat limits, reach anyone else's data, or overload the app. Access can be removed if this happens.
